# Age of Olympus v1.4.0 Stage 4 — Living Pantheon Generations + Navigation 2.0

Replace:
- index.html
- style.css
- game.js

Living Pantheon Generations:
- Other gods and Titans can independently fall in love.
- NPC immortals can marry without player intervention.
- Married gods/Titans can have immortal children.
- Immortal children inherit a divine domain influenced by their parents.
- NPC immortal children age from Divine Infant to Mature Immortal.
- Olympian gods can have demigod children with mortals.
- NPC demigods have divine parent, mortal parent, city, age, fame, reputation, and domain potential.
- Autonomous marriages and children connect to the Family Tree and Divine Houses.
- NPC Divine Houses include both immortal children and demigod branches.
- Major marriages and births create notifications and Chronicle records.
- Player marriage, demigods, immortal children, jealousy, sibling awareness, monster plots, and ascension remain intact.

Navigation 2.0:
- Replaced the crowded bottom bar with seven large categories:
  World, Divine, Heroes, Mythic, Realm, Chronicle, Alerts.
- Tapping a category opens a clean secondary menu.
- Existing systems remain available; they are simply grouped.
- Optimized for iPhone and iPad.
