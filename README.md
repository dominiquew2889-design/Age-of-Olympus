# Age of Olympus v1.4.0 Stage 6 — Fate, Consequences & Generational Stories

Replace:
- index.html
- style.css
- game.js

## New: Living Sagas
Connected events are grouped into named mythological story arcs that can unfold for decades:
- family feuds
- forbidden divine relationships
- succession rivalries
- demigod bloodlines
- prophecy arcs
- Titan-era consequences

## New: Interactive Prophecies
The Moirai can reveal prophecies about divine families, heroes, cities, ascension, and Titans.
You can:
- Encourage Fate
- Defy Fate
- Observe
Your choice changes how likely the prophecy is to be fulfilled or twisted.

## New: Long-Term Consequences
Important player actions can return several years later.
The Living Chronicle links the new consequence back to the original decision and year.

## New: Generational Bloodlines
Divine Houses now feed into a Bloodline system with:
- founders
- descendants
- prestige
- heirs
- immortal and demigod branches

## New: Deeper Demigod Lives
As demigods age they can choose paths such as:
- Heroic Training
- Temple Service
- Scholarship
- Adventure
- Politics

Adult demigods can begin new life chapters such as marriage, heroic schools, city championship, royal service, or seeking immortality.

## New: Autonomous Family Drama
The divine world can independently generate:
- family feuds
- resentment
- succession disputes
- dangerous romantic bonds

These become Alerts, Chronicle entries, and ongoing Sagas.

## Save migration
Stage 6 uses `ageOfOlympusSaveV140S6`.
If a Stage 6 save does not exist, the game can load the Stage 5 save as a migration fallback, so your existing campaign can continue.

All Stage 5 systems remain included: Living Chronicle, After-Action Reports, Navigation 2.0, autonomous Pantheon generations, Divine Houses, marriages, immortal children, demigods, heroes, Titans, Council voting, alerts, and the rest of the game.
