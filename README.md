# Age of Olympus v1.4.0 Stage 9 — Unified Olympus & Divine Favor

Stage 9 synchronizes Divine Family, Family Tree, Divine Houses, demigods, immortal children, NPC descendants, and spouse/family links from one canonical family graph. It also repairs missing player icons and stale child/sibling lists when an existing save loads.

A new Divine > Divine Favor screen gives active ways to earn Favor with cooldowns and progression ranks.

Stage 9 migrates from Stage 8 and older compatible saves.
