# Age of Olympus v1.4.0 Stage 10 — Divine Matchmaking & Immortal Dynasties

Replace:
- index.html
- style.css
- game.js

## Player-Directed Divine Matchmaking
The player can now choose an unmarried god or Titan and propose another immortal as a match.

Acceptance depends on:
- compatibility
- affection
- trust
- attraction
- Prestige
- Divine Favor
- faction alignment
- rivalries
- Titan/Olympian political tension

A match can be Accepted or Rejected.

## Formal Divine Weddings
Accepted betrothals can be turned into formal divine weddings.

A wedding automatically:
- links both spouses in Unified Olympus
- updates the Divine Family Tree
- updates old autonomous marriage systems
- creates a Divine House
- updates Chronicle and Alerts
- affects Titan politics when the marriage crosses Olympian/Titan lines
- feeds Prestige and Favor

## Immortal Children Over Time
Arranged married immortals can naturally have fully immortal children as years pass.

Children receive:
- both parents
- a divine domain influenced by the parents
- Divine Infant life stage
- power, wisdom, loyalty
- Divine House membership
- Unified Family Tree ancestry
- Chronicle birth record
- Alerts
- future access to Living Souls, factions, offices, rivalries, and succession as they grow

## Immortal Dynasties
The Matchmaking page shows:
- arranged betrothals
- pending weddings
- founded Divine Houses
- immortal children
- influence
- unity

## Unified Family Integration
All arranged marriages and children flow through the Stage 9 canonical family system so Family Tree, Divine Houses, Living Souls, children, parents, spouses, and ancestry stay synchronized.

## Save Migration
Stage 10 uses `ageOfOlympusSaveV140S10`.
If it does not exist, the game falls back to Stage 9 and earlier compatible saves.

All Stage 9 systems remain included.
